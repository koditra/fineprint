fineprint

Ever open a document and immediately start skimming?

Same. We made Fine Print at a hackathon for the pile of everyday paperwork that somehow expects everyone to speak fluent legalese.

Drop in a lease, waiver, privacy policy, or whatever you’re trying to get through. Fine Print gives you the gist, translates the confusing bits, and helps you spot dates, costs, and terms worth a second look. Then you can ask follow-up questions without starting over or hunting through the whole document.

Basically, you can:

  • Get the gist — the main points, minus the wall of text.
  • Translate a clause — get a simple explanation of the bit you keep rereading.
  • Know what to double-check — dates, money, and terms that might deserve another look.
  • Keep asking — follow up about the same document in a little back-and-forth chat.

PDF, TXT, Markdown, or pasted text all work. Pick the kind of help you want; Fine Print sends the document to Hack Club AI when you ask for an explanation. It’s a helpful starting point, not a lawyer—so skip anything you don’t want to share.

Made for a hackathon, for everyone who has ever opened a document and thought, “I am not reading all that.”
